Zero to AI Engineer,
a free route for two

From zero to building agents: seven foundation topics (F1–F7) for complete beginners, then eight AI-engineering topics — LLMs, prompting, RAG, knowledge graphs, agents, harnesses, MCP and production. Each topic has its own video, short lessons, coding practice you run right here in the page, a quiz after every sub-topic and a topic test. Pass the test to unlock the next station.

Learning as:

Progress and your code are saved in this browser, separately for each learner. Each of you can also use your own device.

No recording needed: your device reads the narration aloud in time with each video. Choose Telugu + English script with a Telugu (India) voice for Tenglish narration, or the English script with an English (India) voice. These are your device’s own natural voices: on Android/Chrome look for “English (India)” or “తెలుగు (భారతదేశం)”; on Windows use Microsoft Edge, which offers natural India English and Telugu voices; on iPhone add “English (India)” under Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
